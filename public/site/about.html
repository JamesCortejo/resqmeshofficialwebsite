About ResQMesh

A local emergency network for connected response.

ResQMesh helps Valencia City prepare verified civilians, rescuers, mesh devices, and administrators for both everyday connected access and emergency communication disruptions.

The challenge

Why ResQMesh Exists

Emergencies can happen when mobile data, internet service, or regular communication channels are weak or unavailable. ResQMesh was created to give Valencia City a practical way to keep distress information, location details, and responder communication moving through both connected services and nearby mesh nodes.

Communication should not stop when connectivity does.

System capabilities

What ResQMesh Can Do

  • Share distress and locationSend emergency details with available GPS coordinates.
  • Communicate in two modesUse online services or connect through a nearby mesh node.
  • Coordinate emergency responseHelp administrators monitor alerts and organize rescue deployments.
  • Synchronize after outagesMove supported mesh records to the cloud when connectivity returns.
Project goals

Focused on practical emergency readiness.

ResQMesh is designed as a practical capstone system for local registration, online-and-offline communication, and response coordination.

Deployment Focus

Local coverage

Support emergency communication across selected Valencia City areas through online coordination and synchronized mesh devices.

Technology Focus

Resilient access

Use online services during normal conditions and mesh-supported communication to keep essential messaging available when networks are limited.

Mission Focus

Faster coordination

Help administrators monitor emergencies and deploy rescue teams with clearer operational information.

Routing and Map Data

ResQMesh uses openrouteservice to calculate routes, travel distances, and estimated arrival information for emergency response. openrouteservice is provided by the Heidelberg Institute for Geoinformation Technology (HeiGIT), using map data contributed by OpenStreetMap.
